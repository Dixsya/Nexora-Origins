WORLD

Harvest trees and mine gold ore to earn gems, then spend them on tools, torches, flowers, ladders and building materials.

Computer: A/D or ← → to walk, W / ↑ / Space to jump, click to act, keys 1–9 and 0 (or Q / E) to select an item, Enter to chat, F at the door to enter Valley Village. Hold Jump on a ladder to climb.

Phone: use the ◀ ▶ ▲ buttons on screen, tap a tile to act, hold to repeat.

Pickaxe (buy it in the Shop) breaks blocks twice as fast and always drops the block. Fist breaks blocks and harvests trees. Gold ore hides deep in the stone layer. Door: click the wooden door at the start (or press F next to it) to walk into Valley Village. Use the 🌙 button for night mode. Torch glows, Flower grows on dirt, Ladder lets you climb. Black Door blocks the way until you click it with a block selected to open or close it; use Fist or Pickaxe to break it. Bed: place it on a solid block, then stand next to it and press F (or click it with a block selected) at night to sleep until morning. Sleeping also sets your respawn point; use Fist or Pickaxe to break it. Use the 🔊 / 🎵 buttons in the header to toggle sound effects and music. Seeds are planted on top of dirt and grow into trees in 30 seconds.

Quests: open the Quests tab for programming challenges from Zorr. Correct answers give gems and building materials.

Commands: /help · /quests · /home · /sleep · /night · /day · /reset · /world name

Type a different world name above to enter another world. Every name generates its own terrain.
